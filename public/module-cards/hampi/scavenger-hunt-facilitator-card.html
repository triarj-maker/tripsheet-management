Scavenger Hunt - Facilitator Card

1. Snapshot

Use this card only during live execution.
For background, structure details, or deeper interpretation, use the full playbook.

Location: Royal Enclosure, Hampi
Duration: 120 mins
Team Size: 6–8 students
Adult Support: 1 teacher per team
Format: Scavenger hunt / inquiry trail

Student Task:
Find the clue. Capture the evidence. Figure out what it reveals.

Core Frame:
Observation → Evidence → Inference → Interpretation

2. Before You Start

Confirm:

  • Group-wise worksheets are ready.
  • Each group has one assigned teacher.
  • Teachers have phones/cameras for photo evidence.
  • Briefing point, entry gate, and assembly point are fixed.
  • Movement boundaries and no-go zones are clear.
  • Stationed facilitators know their positions.
  • High-supervision groups are identified.
  • First-aid and water plan are in place.

Station facilitators near:

  • Tanks and water structures.
  • Underground chamber.
  • Entrance door / movement-control zones.
  • Elevated platforms.
  • Narrow or crowded areas.

3. Live Flow

A. Brief Students and Teachers . 5 mins

Cover only the essentials:

  • This is a scavenger hunt, but not only a speed game.
  • Students are reading the site like investigators.
  • Every tank, platform, doorway, carving, drain, stone block, and chamber can become evidence.
  • Archaeologists observe first, gather evidence, and make careful inferences.
  • Teachers supervise and capture photo evidence. They do not solve clues.

Must Say:

“Stay with your assigned teacher at all times. No one moves alone.

Do not run. Do not climb on monuments, walls, platforms, boulders, tanks, chambers, or railings.

Do not lean over tank edges, drains, or the underground chamber.

Do not touch, scratch, or lean on carvings or fragile structures.

If you are stuck, ask a stationed facilitator. At the end, every group must assemble at the point marked on your map.”

Heritage Conduct Line:

“We are guests in a protected heritage landscape. Our job is to observe carefully, not disturb the place.”

B. Explain Scoring . 1 min

  • Points come from correct written answers and correct photo evidence.
  • Unclear photos may need explanation.
  • Unsafe behaviour, group separation, climbing, or damaging the site can lose points.
  • Highest score wins.

Must Say:

“We are not only checking whether you found the place. We are checking whether you found the right evidence.”

C. Hand Over Worksheets and Release Groups . 5 mins

  1. Call one group at a time.
  2. Confirm assigned teacher.
  3. Hand over correct group worksheet.
  4. Confirm group name / number.
  5. Tell them to begin with the first challenge page.
  6. Release groups one at a time through the selected entrance.
  7. Leave a small gap between groups.

Do not release all groups together.

D. During the Hunt . 90 mins

Facilitators should clarify without giving answers too quickly.

Use prompts first. Give direct hints only if the group is fully stuck or losing momentum.

Ask if needed:

  • What does the worksheet ask you to find?
  • What evidence proves this is the right structure?
  • What do you see?
  • Is that an observation or an inference?
  • What photo will prove your answer?
  • What does this structure suggest about function or activity?
  • Could there be another explanation?

Intervene immediately if:

  • Students separate from the group.
  • Students run or climb.
  • Students move near unsafe edges.
  • Students touch or damage structures.
  • A teacher is unable to control the group.
  • A group moves outside boundaries.

E. Assemble and Score . 20 mins

  1. Call all groups to the marked assembly point.
  2. Count students and confirm all groups have returned.
  3. Ask teachers to keep photo evidence ready.
  4. Stop all answer editing once scoring begins.
  5. Call one group at a time.
  6. Check written answers and required photos.
  7. Ask for explanation if a photo is unclear.
  8. Record total score.
  9. Announce winner after all groups are checked.

Award points for:

  • Correct location.
  • Correct written answer.
  • Clear photo evidence.
  • Evidence-based explanation.

Deduct / withhold points for:

  • Missing photo evidence.
  • Unsafe conduct.
  • Group separation.
  • Climbing or touching monuments.
  • Guesswork without evidence.
  • Editing answers after scoring begins.

F. Debrief . 3–10 mins

Focus first on teamwork. Connect briefly to the Royal Enclosure only after that.

Teamwork prompts:

  • How did your team decide where to go first?
  • Did everyone get a chance to contribute?
  • Where did your group disagree?
  • How did you resolve that disagreement?
  • What did your team do well?
  • What would you change if you had to do this again?
  • When your group was stuck, what helped you move forward?

Site-learning prompts:

  • Which clue made your group observe most carefully?
  • What evidence helped you feel confident about an answer?
  • What did the site reveal about how this part of Vijayanagara may have functioned?
  • What is one thing we still cannot know for certain from the remains alone?

Closing Line:

“Today was not just about finding answers. It was about how you worked together, handled confusion, and used evidence to make decisions. That is also how real field investigations work.”

4. Materials

  • Group-wise printed worksheets.
  • Pencils or pens.
  • Clipboards, if available.
  • Teacher phone / camera for photo evidence.
  • Scoring sheet.
  • First-aid kit.
  • Drinking water access plan.
