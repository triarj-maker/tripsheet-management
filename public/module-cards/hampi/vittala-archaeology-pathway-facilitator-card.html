Vittala - Archaeology Pathway - Facilitator Card

Use During Live Module

Live execution card. Use for safety, movement, timing, handovers and group management. Academic delivery stays with the Guide / Lead Facilitator as assigned. Read the master playbook before the trip.

Snapshot

Duration: 140 mins

Group: 1 Guide per group of approximately 20 students

Route: Vittala Parking → Buggy Line → Vittala Entrance / Gopura → Kalyana Mantapa → Ranga Mantapa → Stone Chariot → Purandara Dasa Mantapa → Return to Bus

Academic lead: Lead Facilitator at the start; Guide through Gopura, Kalyana and Ranga; Guide / Lead Facilitator at Stone Chariot

Start Checks

  • Guide present and ready to receive the group.
  • School letter / documentation ready to hand over.
  • Buggy movement confirmed.
  • Adults briefed: one adult in each buggy.
  • Headcount completed before movement.
  • Stone Chariot delivery mode confirmed: Guide-led or Lead Facilitator in staggered slots.

Rules to Repeat as Needed

  • Stay with the group and follow the designated route.
  • Remain seated throughout the buggy ride. No standing or rushing.
  • No touching, climbing, leaning or sitting on protected monuments. Do not place bags or objects on them.
  • Musical pillars and other architectural elements must not be tapped, struck or physically tested.
  • At Purandara Dasa Mantapa, students must remain within the designated safe area and away from the water.
  • Facilitators manage movement, positioning, sightlines, behaviour and headcounts while allowing the Guide to deliver without unnecessary interruption.

1. Vittala Parking & Buggy Transfer

Time: 30 mins

Lead Facilitator does: Gather the full group in a shaded area, brief buggy conduct and explain that everyone will regroup near the Vittala entrance.

Key message:

“Remain seated at all times in the buggy. When you reach the other end, stay with the adult accompanying your buggy and move to the designated gathering area.”

Facilitator does:

  • Conduct headcount before boarding.
  • Hand the school’s required documentation to the Guide.
  • Keep students in a controlled line.
  • Board approximately 10 students per buggy with one adult in each buggy.
  • Prevent students from rushing ahead or leaving the line.
  • At the drop-off point, move students safely to the designated gathering area without blocking visitor traffic.

2. Vittala Entrance — Gopura

Time: 15 mins

Lead Facilitator opens the academic pathway before groups split.

Land two ideas:

Observation = what you can actually see.

Interpretation = what the evidence might mean.

Key line:

“Through our time at Vittala, keep asking yourself two questions: What can I see? And what might that evidence mean?”

Then introduce the Guides, divide into guide groups and hand over clearly.

Facilitator does:

  • Keep each guide group together and out of visitor flow.
  • Position students so they can see the Gopura and hear the Guide.
  • Manage behaviour and movement without taking over the academic discussion.

3. Kalyana Mantapa

Time: 10 mins

Guide covers: Yali, mounted warlords, observation and interpretation of sculpture.

Facilitator does:

  • Position students where the Yali and riders are clearly visible.
  • Stop touching, leaning or climbing immediately.
  • Keep the group compact and support the Guide’s sightlines.
  • Avoid adding extra questions unless the Guide needs operational help.

4. Ranga Mantapa

Time: 15 mins

Guide covers: Musical-pillar claim, pillar form, vibration and resonance, stone properties, sculptures, reported sounds and historical knowledge.

Before the section:

Confirm the Guide has the tuning fork with rubber activator and the required visual aids.

Facilitator does:

  • Stand near likely touch points.
  • Prevent touching, tapping, striking or testing any pillar.
  • Keep students within the permitted viewing position and avoid crowding the columns.
  • Help the Guide display visual aids where the full group can see them.

5. Stone Chariot

Time: 10 mins

Led by: Guide or Lead Facilitator

Before the section:

If Lead Facilitator-led, sequence guide groups into staggered slots.

If Guide-led, each guide group continues independently.

During the investigation:

  • After the monolith demonstration, divide the group into teams of approximately 5.
  • Give one A5 Investigation Card to each team.
  • Where four teams are formed, duplicate one of the three claim cards.
  • Allow 2–3 minutes for the team investigation.
  • Students investigate orally. No writing is required.
  • Keep students within permitted viewing areas and prevent touching the monument.
  • Do not reveal answers. Use only simple evidence prompts if students are stuck.

Regroup:

  • Each team reports its claim, conclusion and strongest piece of evidence.
  • Ensure every team is heard before moving on.

6. Purandara Dasa Mantapa

Time: 30 mins

Academic content: None. This is a recreational break.

Facilitator says:

“We have finished our Vittala session. You now have about 30 minutes here to relax and take a break. Stay within the area designated by your facilitators and do not move towards the water or away from the group.”

Facilitator does:

  • Keep students within the designated safe area.
  • Monitor students continuously.
  • Prevent movement towards unsafe areas or the water’s edge.
  • Allow students to rest and have water.
  • Give a clear warning before the break ends.
  • Before departure, regroup, conduct a headcount and check belongings.

7. Return Journey — Purandara Dasa Mantapa to Bus

Time: 30 mins

Before moving:

  • Ensure all students have their belongings.
  • Conduct a headcount.
  • Position facilitators at the front, middle and rear as appropriate.

Buggy boarding:

  • Maintain the student line.
  • Board approximately 10 students per buggy.
  • Ensure one adult is present in each buggy.
  • Keep students seated throughout the transfer.

At Vittala Parking:

  • Reform the group immediately.
  • Move students directly towards the bus.
  • Prevent students from dispersing around the parking area.

Before bus departure:

  • Ask students to check journals, pens, water bottles and personal belongings.
  • Conduct the final headcount once students are seated.
  • Confirm the full group is present before the bus departs.
